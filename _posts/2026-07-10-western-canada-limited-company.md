__Result:__ Go with Alberta.
This post first recommended British Columbia (July 2021), because anyone could incorporate there self-service.
Five years later (July 2026), BC requires a BC Services Card for most filings, so non-residents are forced to use a registered agent.
Alberta always required one, so BC lost its edge and Alberta wins on everything else.

## What Changed
The old [Corporate Online](https://www.corporateonline.gov.bc.ca/) is being merged into
[BC Registries and Online Services](https://www.bcregistry.gov.bc.ca/en-CA/).
On the surface that is good.
The bad news: you need a [BC Services Card](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/government-id/bc-services-card)
to do most things, which means you must be a resident.

Anyone used to be able to use Corporate Online and stay entirely self-service.
Now non-residents have to have a registered agent.
British Columbia made itself less competitive, and with everything else going for it, Alberta is the logical choice.

- Pay a registered agent to file your Alberta annual return: <https://theregistrationcenter.ca/product/annual-return-filing/>
- Save on corporate income taxes: <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/canada/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income>

## The Problem
My minimum bar for most strategies: residency and citizenship should not matter.

Alberta and British Columbia both allow incorporators AND directors to be non-residents of Canada.
Nova Scotia does too, but has [high annual renewal fees](https://www.novascotia.ca/incorporate-limited-company).

## Why Western Canada
- __Alberta__: No provincial sales tax. Like other energy exporters, the province pays for much of its services with energy royalties.
- __British Columbia__: Some useful digital sales tax exclusions.
  - [Software consulting services and database access](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/pst/publications/software#internet-services) pay no provincial sales tax.
  - [Website subscriptions with text only, or text and still photos](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/pst/publications/telecommunication-services#website-subscriptions) are not taxable sales.
- __Both__: Once you incorporate in a member province, other Western Canadian markets are easy to access: <https://www.newwestpartnershiptrade.ca/the_agreement.htm>

## What I Did

__Get an address.__ For about 15CAD a month, [Anytime Mailbox]({{ site.program.business.anytimemb }})
or [iPostal1]({{ site.program.business.ipostal1 }}) gives you a remotely managed address in Alberta or British Columbia.
That covers the requirement for an office and mailing address in your province.

__Notify under the Investment Canada Act.__ A non-resident creating a Canadian resident business has to notify
_Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada_ by
[filling out ISED-ISDE3675E](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/investment-canada-act/en/investment-canada-act/forms)
and emailing it back.
Investments over 1 billion CAD have to wait for their review.

__Get a personal tax number.__ To file with the Canada Revenue Agency for your new company, you need an ITN.
Fill out and return the
[_Application for a Canada Revenue Agency Individual Tax Number (ITN) for Non-Residents_](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t1261.html).
Include a notarized copy of your passport with a wet signature from the notary.
I did this at my local UPS store for $5.

__Incorporate (the July 2021 BC process):__

1. If you want a real name instead of a number from the province, reserve one with the
[Name Request tool](https://www.bcregistry.ca/namerequest) for
[$30](https://www.corporateonline.gov.bc.ca/WebHelp/fee_schedule.htm).
It takes a few days to process before the next step.
1. Go through the [BC Registry Services incorporation wizard](https://www.bcregistry.ca/corporateonline/colin/accesstransaction/menu.do?action=overview&filingTypeCode=ICORP&from=main) for [$350](https://www.corporateonline.gov.bc.ca/WebHelp/fee_schedule.htm).
1. Add your name and the business name to the
[example incorporation agreement and example incorporation articles](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/employment-business-and-economic-development/business-management/permits-licences-and-registration/registries-other-assets/samp_02_-_incorporation_agreement_adopting_table_1.pdf).
Do not forget section 9.10's proxy designation.
Convert them to PDF and get them [notarized online with Notary Pro](https://www.notarypro.ca/notarize-online/book-now/).

## Ongoing Upkeep
These come from the [BC Business Corporations Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/02057_00):
<sup>[1](https://www.barbeau.co/uncategorized/annual-requirements-british-columbia-companies/)</sup>

- __Annual General Meeting__: Hold one and record notes. For a one-person company, that can be you writing down actions taken or planned in the minute book.
A unanimous resolution of all voting shareholders (you) can waive it.
- __Financial statements__: Directors (you) approve, sign, produce, and publish them before the AGM,
unless all voting and non-voting shareholders pass a unanimous resolution.
- __Auditor__: One must be appointed, unless a unanimous resolution waives it.
- __Annual Report__: File it [with BC Registries](https://www.corporateonline.gov.bc.ca/WebHelp/overview_annbc.htm) within 2 months of your incorporation anniversary.
- __Taxes__: File your [T2 and tax calculation paperwork](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/corporate) with the Canada Revenue Agency.
I use <https://netfilet2.taxtron.ca/>.
First you have to file an individual $0 tax return to get into the CRA's system and be self-serve.
