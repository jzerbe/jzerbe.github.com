## The Problem
Every organization will leak your data eventually.
The profit motive can get in the way of security, and they may not pay enough to hire quality people.
So only give your data to services you absolutely need.
Remember: if the service is free, you and your data are the product being sold.

Then there are all the organizations you give data to without knowing it.
A [data broker](https://www.gartner.com/en/information-technology/glossary/data-broker) makes money
by collecting your data, and lots of other people's, then selling it.
Here is how I keep my data out of their leaky databases.

## What I Did: Prevention

### Chrome

1. Open Settings.
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_settings.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
2. Open the "Privacy and security" tab.
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_privacy_and_security.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
3. Click the first item, "Delete browsing data". Click "Delete data" to clear everything your browser stores
for websites, data brokers included. This logs you out of every website.
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_delete_data.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
4. Click "Third-party cookies". Scroll down and select "Block third-party cookies".
Turn off "Allow related sites to see your activity in the group". Turn on "Send a Do Not Track request".
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_third_party_cookies.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
5. Open "Ad Privacy" and turn off all this crap.
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_ad_privacy.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
6. Last, open "Site settings", scroll down to "Embedded Content", and select "Sites are blocked ...".
   <img src="/assets/posts/chrome_embedded_content.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />

### Firefox

1. Open Firefox settings from the menu in the top right.
   <img src="/assets/posts/firefox_settings.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
2. Select the "Privacy & Security" tab on the left.
Firefox's standard protections cover pretty much everything we did in Chrome.
   <img src="/assets/posts/firefox_privacy_security.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
3. Scroll past the "Standard" Browser Privacy section and turn on all of the "Website Privacy Preferences".
   <img src="/assets/posts/firefox_website_privacy.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />

### Gmail

1. Click the gear icon in the top right corner, then "See all settings".
   <img src="/assets/posts/gmail_settings.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
2. In the "General" tab, scroll down and select "Ask before displaying external images".
This blocks [tracking pixels](https://en.ryte.com/wiki/Tracking_Pixel/).
   <img src="/assets/posts/gmail_images.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
3. Scroll further and uncheck "Turn on smart features and personalization in other Google products".
This stops Google from scraping your emails to suggest features and products.
   <img src="/assets/posts/gmail_smart_features.png" alt="drawing" class="image fit" />
4. Scroll to the bottom and click "Save Changes".
