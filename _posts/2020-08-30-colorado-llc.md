__Result:__ A single-member Colorado LLC costs about $225 to $375 to set up and $10 a year to keep in good standing.
If the LLC's business is _Investing and Lending_ and your taxable income is under the QBI limits,
the interest income running through it can qualify for the 20% Qualified Business Income deduction.
Colorado beat Nevada for me on filing fees and crypto rules, even though Nevada has no income tax.

## The Problem
[Real wages have stagnated](https://www.pewresearch.org/fact-tank/2018/08/07/for-most-us-workers-real-wages-have-barely-budged-for-decades/),
and regular employees pay a [larger share of national taxes](https://www.cbpp.org/research/federal-tax/policy-basics-where-do-federal-tax-revenues-come-from)
than [shell companies for the rich](https://www.icij.org/investigations/panama-papers/).
So why not play the same games until the legislation catches up?

To be clear, I do not support an unfair tax system.
I want regular people to use the same loopholes while we work on reversing the
[Citizens United](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/citizens-united-explained) mess.

## What I Set Up
A [Single Member LLC](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies).
It has the easiest paperwork and is
[usually the best option for the 20% Qualified Business Income deduction](https://www.thetaxadviser.com/issues/2020/mar/optimal-choice-entity-qbi-deduction.html).

__Creating it:__

1. [$50 to create in Colorado](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html).
1. $150 to $300 for an hour or two of a lawyer's time to write a manager-managed operating agreement. Or pay me for one of mine.
1. [$25 to notarize the operating agreement](https://www.notarize.com/pricing).
1. Optional: $10 to $12 a month for a mailbox with [Anytime Mailbox]({{ site.program.business.anytimemb }}) or [iPostal1]({{ site.program.business.ipostal1 }}).
This shields your home address and can put you in a lower sales tax, less regulated municipality (unincorporated Jefferson, Gilpin, or Weld county).

__Taxes:__ You file them with your normal personal income taxes.

> If a single-member LLC does not elect to be treated as a corporation, the LLC is a "disregarded entity," and the LLC's activities should be reflected on its owner's federal tax return.

<https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies>

The catch: the LLC's taxable income is taxed at the rate where you live.
If you live in a high tax jurisdiction, you need to get more creative.
More on that in my [family wealth office](/2021/03/family-wealth-office/) write-up.

__Upkeep:__

1. File a [$10 annual report](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/business/FAQs/reports.html).
1. [Apply for an EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)
and keep a bank account separate from your personal money.
I like the [Fidelity Account for Businesses](https://www.fidelity.com/open-account/fidelity-account-for-businesses).
The downside: Fidelity (and American Express) crack down on
[physical addresses from a Commercial Mail Receiving Agent](https://help.virtualpostmail.com/article/224-2019-02-21-proof-of-address-for-business-bank-accounts).
Other banks let you use a virtual mail provider as your physical address, which matters for the reasons below.
If your bank cares, [check whether your address gets flagged as a Commercial Mail Receiving Agent](https://tools.usps.com/zip-code-lookup.htm?byaddress).
1. Set up quarterly electronic estimated tax payments for the
[US federal government](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashleaebeling/2014/04/08/the-easy-way-to-make-estimated-tax-payments/)
and [your formation state](https://www.colorado.gov/pacific/tax/business-income-estimated-payments),
if you have no other withholding (like a W-2 employer).
1. File [Beneficial Ownership Information with FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi), or hire a registered agent to do it.

__Why the interest income qualifies:__ If your
[taxable income is under $157,500 single or $315,000 married filing jointly](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/tax-cuts-and-jobs-act-provision-11011-section-199a-qualified-business-income-deduction-faqs),
and the business purpose is _Investing and Lending_ (this one is),
then interest income through the LLC is Qualified Business Income.
The [interest income is _properly allocable to a trade or business_](https://www.law.cornell.edu/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-17049456-273802394&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:B:part:VI:section:199A).

## Why the Address Matters
The address on your banking and letterhead can be the difference between paying 10% on your QBI and paying nothing.
Think California vs Nevada, or Oregon vs Washington.

An _Investing and Lending_ business with no employees, no sales, no services, and no tangible property
should use an address with low or no income tax. Occupation, sales, and use taxes do not matter here.

Everyone talks about Delaware (zero sales tax).
For this use, Nevada (zero income tax) makes more sense, and
[Nevada does not tax services](https://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:y4jBz1TcnR8J:https://tax.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/taxnvgov/Content/OnLineServices/Basic%2520Training-revised.ppt+&cd=13&hl=en&ct=clnk&gl=us).

## Why I Still Picked Colorado
- __Fees__: [Nevada business filing fees](https://www.nvsos.gov/sos/businesses/commercial-recordings/forms-fees)
are much higher than [Colorado's](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html).
At lower income levels, the fees cost as much as Colorado's 4.5% income tax.
- __Crypto__: [Colorado's cryptocurrency rules are more progressive](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2019/3/colorado-digital-token-act-exempts-certain-cryptocurrency-transactions-from-colorado-securities-laws),
which I need for my LLC's investments.

## Moving Assets In
To save on taxes, you transfer accounts and assets to the LLC as a
[capital contribution](https://www.legalzoom.com/articles/how-to-add-capital-contributions-to-an-llc).
Fiduciaries usually want:

- [Articles of organization and/or a Certificate of Good Standing](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/business/FAQs/certGoodStanding.html) from the secretary of state where you formed
- The operating agreement, including beneficial owners (remember getting that notarized?)
- Government ID for every beneficial owner with more than 25%
- A bank statement in the company's name, with the business address
- A detailed description of the business activity (for anti-money laundering checks)

Depending on where you live and how much you move in, the tax savings can cover all the startup fees in the first month.

## Lessons for Later
There is no public market for LLC membership interests.
So [gifting membership interests to your heirs while you are still the manager means big asset write-downs](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/personal-finance/071514/using-llc-estate-planning.asp#how-a-family-llc-works)
for estate purposes.
