__Result:__ A foreigner can get a Canadian bank account by walking into a branch with a passport, a driver license, and a reason.
Mine was running a Canadian business.
I opened a BMO account near the Calgary airport and put in enough ($3k) to skip their fees.
The plan from here: build credit history for a few years, then move to Wealthsimple and close the BMO account.

## The Problem
I diversify my investments by geography, market cycle, and industry.
I do the same with my money and my access to financial services.
[Wise]({{ site.program.business.wise }}) gives me flexibility right away,
but it does not add new jurisdictions where I can open a financial services account.

For bankers, running a Canadian business is usually enough reason for a personal Canadian account.

## What I Did

__Opened a bank account.__ Canadian banking is an oligopoly.
Most banks with physical branches charge fees for everything, and as a foreigner you have to start at one of them.
You walk into a branch, show enough ID (passport plus driver license), and give them a good reason to bank you (business owner).

I scheduled an appointment online at the [BMO near the Calgary airport](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5PxKWa5UyWr6D63G7) and did all of the above.
I transferred in enough cash ($3k) via Wise to avoid fees on their [Plus plan](https://www.bmo.com/en-ca/main/personal/bank-accounts/chequing-accounts/).
It was the lowest tier where a balance waives the fees.

Bank your business with [Wealthsimple]({{ site.program.business.wealthsimple }}). So much better.
Once you have personal banking and credit history, move that to Wealthsimple too.

__Started a credit history.__ You need a Canadian address and phone number.

- __Address__: a coworking space, an [Anytime Mailbox]({{ site.program.business.anytimemb }}) or [iPostal1]({{ site.program.business.ipostal1 }}) address, a hotel, or a colleague's apartment.
- __Phone__: With no credit history, you need a prepaid plan. <https://www.koodomobile.com/en/prepaid-plans> and <https://shop.freedommobile.ca/en-CA/prepaid-plans> are your nationwide options.
Most financial institutions crack down on VoIP numbers.
- __First credit card__: With zero Canadian credit history, the bank where you opened your chequing account is probably one of the few that will consider a credit card or revolving line of credit.

Other routes to a first account:

- Some multinational banks will open an account for you in your destination country. HSBC used to do this more often.
- If you already have an American Express relationship, use it to open your first card: <https://www.americanexpress.com/us/customer-service/global-card-relationship/>

## What Is Next
After the accounts have been open a few years, you can open accounts elsewhere. Again, I highly suggest Wealthsimple.
Once the Wealthsimple account is open, close the high-cost, low-value BMO account and card.
