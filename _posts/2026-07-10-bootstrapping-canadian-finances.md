__Result:__ It worked. By October 2026 I was entirely off BMO and fully on Wealthsimple.
My Alberta company runs day-to-day chequeing on [Wise]({{ site.program.business.wise }}),
and its unused cash sits in non-US, and some non-North American, ETFs in a Wealthsimple corporate account.
The way in: walk into a big bank branch with a passport, a driver license, and a reason.
Mine was running a Canadian business.

_First written July 2026, updated October 2026._

## The Problem
I diversify my investments by geography, market cycle, and industry.
I do the same with my money and my access to financial services.
[Wise]({{ site.program.business.wise }}) gives me flexibility right away,
but it does not add new jurisdictions where I can open a financial services account.

As a foreigner with no Canadian credit history, the good options will not take you yet.

__Hypothesis:__ Start at one of the big fee-heavy banks, where running a Canadian business is reason enough to bank you,
use it to build a Canadian history, then move everything to [Wealthsimple]({{ site.program.business.wealthsimple }}) and close the bank.

## The Solution I Tried

### 1. Open a bank account
Canadian banking is an oligopoly.
Most banks with physical branches charge fees for everything, and as a foreigner you have to start at one of them.
You walk into a branch, show enough ID (passport plus driver license), and give them a good reason to bank you (business owner).

I scheduled an appointment online at the [BMO near the Calgary airport](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5PxKWa5UyWr6D63G7) and did all of the above.
I transferred in 3,000 CAD via Wise, the minimum daily balance that waives the
[Plus plan's 12.95 CAD monthly fee](https://www.bmo.com/en-ca/main/personal/bank-accounts/chequing-accounts/).
It was the lowest tier where a balance waives the fee.

### 2. Start a credit history
You need a Canadian address and phone number.

- __Address__: a coworking space, an [Anytime Mailbox]({{ site.program.business.anytimemb }}) or [iPostal1]({{ site.program.business.ipostal1 }}) address, a hotel, or a colleague's apartment.
- __Phone__: With no credit history, you need a prepaid plan. [Koodo](https://www.koodomobile.com/en/prepaid-plans) and [Freedom](https://shop.freedommobile.ca/en-CA/prepaid-plans) are your nationwide options.
Most financial institutions crack down on VoIP numbers.
- __First credit card__: With zero Canadian credit history, the bank where you opened your chequing account is probably one of the few that will consider a credit card or revolving line of credit.

### 3. Move everything to Wealthsimple
Personal banking moved to Wealthsimple, and I closed the BMO account and card.

On the business side, the company that is now [Vraid Systems Limited of Alberta](/2021/03/family-wealth-office/) splits the job:

- __Day to day__: chequeing on Wise, which handles multiple currencies.
- __Unused cash__: a [Wealthsimple corporate account](https://wealthsimple.com/en-ca/accounts/corporate-accounts)
holding non-US and some non-North American ETFs. Same diversification idea: not everything in one geography.

## Other Routes to a First Account

- If you already have an American Express relationship, use it to open your first card through the
[Global Card Relationship program](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/customer-service/global-card-relationship/).
- Some multinational banks will open an account for you in your destination country.
HSBC used to be the go-to for Canada, but [RBC bought HSBC Bank Canada in March 2024](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/rbc-completes-acquisition-of-hsbc-bank-canada-849432219.html).
