__Result:__ It failed.
A vehicle gets registered where it lives, not where its owner would like it to live.
A Montana-registered company car, driven by a Colorado resident and kept in Colorado, has to be registered in Colorado.
Wyoming will cite you too if you work there on out-of-state plates without a permit.
The perks below only work if the vehicle is actually based there.

## The Problem
Registering a vehicle costs money every year, and the rules swing a lot by location.
If you move around, or own a business in another state, it looks like you get some choice in the matter.

__Hypothesis:__ Register the vehicle where the fringe benefits are best,
like Montana's permanent registration and zero sales tax, through a company based there.

## The Solution I Tried
I researched the best spots in North America to register a vehicle, then looked at registering a company car through my Montana company.

### Canada

- __British Columbia__: Used to hold the title for the lowest registration fees in Canada, at
[$40-100/year](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.irponline.org/resource/resmgr/fee_schedules/bc_feechart_11.29.12.pdf).
- __Ontario__: Since March 13, 2022, [renewing](https://www.ontario.ca/page/driver-and-vehicle-fees)
a passenger vehicle plate costs __$0__. You still
[renew every one or two years](https://globalnews.ca/news/8636625/ontario-licence-plate-sticker-renewal-cancelled-refunds), just without a sticker.

### United States

- __Alaska__: Permanent registration (pay one fee, never again) for
[vehicles 8 years or older in participating boroughs](https://dmv.alaska.gov/vehicle-services/permanent-registration-for-trailers-and-older-vehicles/).
Residents also get the [Permanent Fund Dividend](https://pfd.alaska.gov/): $3k for 2022.
- __Montana__: Permanent registration (pay one fee, never again) for
[light vehicles 11 years or older](https://mvdmt.gov/vehicle-registration/light-vehicle-registration-and-fees/),
as long as ownership does not change.
No sales tax, so you save on the purchase too. Also also:
[low registration fees](https://leg.mt.gov/content/publications/fiscal/2023-Interim/Aug-2021/Vehicle-Fee-Brochure.pdf),
especially in Flathead County.

### Central America

- __Panama__: Modern, friendly, and a strong democracy (from a memory of a recent autocrat).
[VAT is 7%](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/panama/corporate/other-taxes), plus a
[5% excise tax on cars](https://kpmg.com/us/en/home/insights/2022/06/tnf-panama-exemption-from-selective-consumption-tax-for-electric-vehicles.html).
Registration is [$20 for the inspection and $20 for the municipal paperwork](https://www.chasingpanama.blog/2019/12/04/vehicle-registration-in-panama/).
Registering [through an entity](https://www.relofirm.com/corporation_vehicle_registration/) adds another $100.

## Why It Failed
Every state I looked at keys registration to where the vehicle is kept and driven, and who drives it.

__Colorado:__

- The DMV's position is simple:
["If it is kept at a Colorado residence and driven in the state, it must be registered in Colorado."](https://www.coloradopolitics.com/2026/07/22/colorado-drivers-tap-montana-loophole-to-avoid-high-vehicle-registration-and-tax-costs/)
- You count as a Colorado resident for registration if you
[own or operate a business in Colorado, are employed in Colorado, or live here 90 days](https://www.larimer.gov/clerk/vehiclelicensing/residency).
- [Nonresident businesses have to register vehicles kept in Colorado](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-3-103) and pay the same fees as residents.
A business vehicle used mainly for business gets
[registered where it is principally operated and maintained](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-6-139).
- The [commuter exemption](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-3-103) only covers a nonresident who drives in daily from a home in another state.
A Colorado resident does not qualify.
- Colorado [use tax still applies](https://tax.colorado.gov/sites/tax/files/documents/GIL-08-037.pdf) to a vehicle bought out of state and used here.
- Penalties: a [traffic infraction](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-3-103), late fees,
back ownership taxes, and a [$500 civil penalty](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_42-6-139) for knowing violations.

__Wyoming:__ a nonresident working in Wyoming has to
[register in Wyoming or buy a temporary permit](https://pinedaleonline.com/news/2018/05/Infoonvehicleregistr.htm),
$50 a month for up to 120 days.
Daily commuters from Colorado and Montana are the exception.

__Everyone else is catching on.__ In 2026 California
[charged 14 people](https://www.theautopian.com/states-are-finally-cracking-down-on-the-montana-plate-loophole-by-charging-14-people-with-1-8-million-in-tax-evasion)
over Montana plates, and Utah set up data sharing with Montana to find them.

## What Is Left
Register where the vehicle actually lives.
Montana's permanent registration and no sales tax are a real perk for a vehicle based in Montana.
A company vehicle that genuinely operates out of another state can stay registered there.
One parked in Colorado cannot.
