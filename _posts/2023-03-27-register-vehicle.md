__Result:__ If your car is old enough, Montana and Alaska win: pay one fee and never register again.
Ontario charges $0 but still makes you renew every year.
In Panama, registration runs $40.

## The Problem
Registering a vehicle costs money every year, and the rules swing a lot depending on where you register it.
If you move around, you get some choice in the matter.
So I looked for the spots in North America with the best odd fringe benefits.

## What I Found

### Canada

- __British Columbia__: Used to hold the title for the lowest registration fees in Canada, at
[$40-100/year](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.irponline.org/resource/resmgr/fee_schedules/bc_feechart_11.29.12.pdf).
- __Ontario__: As of March 13 2023, [registering](https://www.ontario.ca/page/driver-and-vehicle-fees)
a passenger vehicle costs __$0__. You still have to renew online every year.

### United States

- __Alaska__: Permanent registration (pay one fee, never again) for
[passenger vehicles 8 years or older in participating boroughs](https://dmv.alaska.gov/vehicle-services/permanent-registration-for-trailers-and-older-vehicles/).
Residents also get the [Permanent Fund Dividend](https://pfd.alaska.gov/): $3k for 2022.
- __Montana__: Permanent registration (pay one fee, never again) for
[passenger vehicles 11 years or older, statewide](https://dojmt.gov/driving/vehicle-title-and-registration/#lightvehiclepr).
No sales tax, so you save on the purchase too. Also also:
[low registration fees](https://leg.mt.gov/content/publications/fiscal/2023-Interim/Aug-2021/Vehicle-Fee-Brochure.pdf),
especially in Flathead County.

### Central America

- __Panama__: Modern, friendly, and a strong democracy (from a memory of a recent autocrat).
[VAT is 7%](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/panama/corporate/other-taxes), plus a
[5% excise tax on cars](https://kpmg.com/us/en/home/insights/2022/06/tnf-panama-exemption-from-selective-consumption-tax-for-electric-vehicles.html).
Registration is [$20 for the inspection and $20 for the municipal paperwork](https://www.chasingpanama.blog/2019/12/04/vehicle-registration-in-panama/).
Registering [through an entity](https://www.relofirm.com/corporation_vehicle_registration/) adds another $100.
